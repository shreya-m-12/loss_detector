Why Two Separate Files?

Keeping transactions and operating expenses in separate tables prevents monthly overheads (like monthly store rent or electricity) from being duplicated across thousands of SKU sale rows, eliminating double-counting errors.

Validation Enforcements:

  • Strict ISO YYYY-MM-DD date format.
  • Mathematical consistency: quantity * price - discount == sales_revenue.
  • No negative values or returns (strictly positive quantity).
  • Inventory snapshot captures closing stock without sum distortions.
