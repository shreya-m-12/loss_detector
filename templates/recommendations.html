Action Playbook by Rule Classification

Standard operating procedures for each flagged category
Rule Primary Trigger Store Owner Action Checklist
R1: Loss-Making Cost > Revenue Audit supplier wholesale price changes, check promotional markdowns, review cashier overrides.
R2: Low-Margin 0 < Margin < 10% Test 3-5% price hike, bundle with high-margin items, check shipping or distributor handling fees.
R3: Declining Sales Decline > 20% vs prior sales month Verify stock availability during month, inspect shelf positioning, check competitor local offers.
R4: Expense Surge Overhead surge > 30% vs prior month Inspect invoice vouchers for one-off charges, check electricity meter readings, review maintenance bills.
R5: Inventory Risk Closing stock > 3x trailing 3-month sales Halt reorders, inspect perishable expiry dates, launch bundle or clearance promotions to free working capital.
R6: Discount Risk Discount margin drop > 15 points Cap discount thresholds to protect 15% minimum margin, shift from flat discounts to volume tiers.
